Ad Tech · Classical ML

Real-time bidding: bid filtering & floor-price optimization

  • 10%+ revenue uplift
  • 40% low-value traffic filtered
  • 30% infra cost cut

Challenge Price ad inventory well and cut wasteful traffic across 50B+ bid requests a day from 100+ bidders, inside a hard latency budget.

Approach & outcome
  • Architected a real-time floor-pricing engine: a multi-armed bandit layer on top of CatBoost base-price regression.
  • Built a CatBoost response-probability filter at 1 ms p99, with a dynamic threshold that caps revenue loss at ~1%.
  • Validated through closed-loop A/B testing with continuous recalibration, for a sustained 10%+ revenue uplift.

CatBoost · Multi-armed bandits · A/B testing

Generative AI · Agents

Multi-agent AI system for support automation

  • 500K users / month
  • 70% resolved unescalated

Challenge Automate high-volume customer support without losing the safety net of human escalation.

Approach & outcome
  • Architected a production multi-agent system on LangGraph: an intent router delegating to FAQ, recommendation, and ticketing agents.
  • Built structured memory, state management, and failure recovery for long-running conversations.
  • Instrumented evaluation and observability with LangFuse so quality is measured, not assumed.

LangGraph · LangChain · LangFuse

Generative AI · SLM fine-tuning

Resume intelligence: CV parsing & JD relevance scoring

  • 2× lower serving cost
  • 86 / 83% parsing P / R
  • 84 / 88% scoring P / R

Challenge Replace a frontier-LLM system with self-hosted models to keep candidate data private, while holding quality and cutting cost.

Approach & outcome
  • Distilled two small language models (QLoRA/PEFT) from human-validated frontier-LLM labels.
  • Parsing: fine-tuned Qwen3-0.6B to extract structured CV fields.
  • Relevance: fine-tuned Qwen3-1.7B to score JD–resume fit with an explanation.
  • Served with vLLM on AWS at half the cost of the previous system.

Qwen3 · QLoRA · PEFT · vLLM · AWS

Generative Video · Computer Vision

Expression & pose transfer: talking-head animation

  • 47% lower per-clip latency
  • 170s → 90s per clip
  • 14B model on 4 GPUs

Challenge Turn a single image into a realistic talking video, fast enough to be a product rather than a demo.

Approach & outcome
  • Architected an end-to-end GPU pipeline: MediaPipe landmarks, face-swap, and Wan2.2-Animate diffusion conditioned on body pose and facial features.
  • Earlier, trained a talking-head model (TS-Net) from scratch with DDP on 8 GPUs over ~10 hours of video.
  • Deployed the 14B model with FSDP-sharded DiT/T5 and DeepSpeed Ulysses; generated at half frame rate and used RIFE interpolation to cut latency 47%.

Wan2.2-Animate · FSDP · DeepSpeed Ulysses · DDP · MediaPipe · RIFE

Automated forecasting pipeline with drift-triggered retraining

Marketing · Survival Analysis

Email send-time optimization

  • +1.5% open rate

Challenge Decide when to send each email so that it lands when the recipient is most likely to open it.

Approach & outcome
  • Framed engagement as a time-to-event problem rather than a plain classification task.
  • Modelled time-to-open with a Cox proportional-hazards model.
  • Used the fitted hazard to choose send times, lifting open rate by 1.5%.

Survival analysis · Cox PH · Python
